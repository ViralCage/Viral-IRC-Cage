05 / How to play

Game Rules

Bagh-Chal (Tigers and Goats)

  • One player controls 4 tigers; the other controls up to 20 goats. Goats move first.
  • During the placement phase, the goat player places one goat on an empty intersection per turn. Tigers may move along a legal connected line to an adjacent empty intersection, or capture a goat by jumping over it to an empty connected intersection when the board lines permit it.
  • After all 20 goats have been placed, the goat player moves one goat per turn along a legal line to an adjacent empty intersection.
  • A tiger wins by capturing 5 goats. A goat wins by blocking all 4 tigers so none has a legal move.
  • Pieces may only move along lines drawn on the board. A piece cannot move to an unconnected point or onto an occupied point. A captured goat is removed from play.

Tic-Tac-Toe

Two players take turns placing X and O in empty squares on a 3×3 grid. The first to make a horizontal, vertical or diagonal line of three wins. If all squares fill without a line, the game is a draw.

Dots & Boxes

Players take turns drawing one horizontal or vertical line between adjacent dots. Completing the fourth side of a box claims that box and gives the player another turn. When no lines remain, the player with the most boxes wins.

Four Connect

Players take turns dropping a piece into a column. Pieces occupy the lowest available space. The first player to connect four pieces horizontally, vertically or diagonally wins. If the board fills with no four-in-a-row, the result is a draw.

Chess

White moves first. Players alternate one legal move at a time. The aim is to checkmate the opponent’s king. Standard chess piece movement, check, checkmate, castling, en passant, promotion, stalemate and draw rules apply. A move that leaves your own king in check is illegal.

Checkers

Players move diagonally on playable squares. Ordinary pieces move forward; kings may move diagonally forward or backward. Captures are made by jumping an opponent’s piece into an empty square beyond it. Captured pieces are removed. The objective is to capture or block all opposing pieces. If the game client enforces mandatory captures or a particular regional rule set, follow the rule indicator shown in that game.

The exact game modes, available options and any timed-match settings are determined by the controls shown in the game. If a game screen displays a specific rule or mode variation, that in-game rule applies to that match.
06 / Help

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to create an account?

Guest-style play is supported. The game may create an anonymous Firebase session identifier for online features; this is not the same as registering with an email and password.

Why does my nickname come back?

Your nickname may be saved in local storage on that browser. Clear the site’s stored data to remove the locally saved nickname.

Can I play without voice or video?

Yes. Voice/video calls are optional. Do not grant microphone or camera permission if you do not want to use those features.

Why did my game disconnect?

Online play depends on your connection, the other player’s connection, browser settings and third-party infrastructure. Rejoin the room if the game allows it. A disconnect may prevent recovery of the match.

Are matches and nicknames public?

Nicknames and match/room data may be shared with the other participant and processed by the online game infrastructure. Do not put private or identifying information in a nickname.

Can I report cheating or abuse?

Use the contact route published on viralcage.com and include the nickname, room/game details and approximate time. Share only information needed to explain the issue.

Can a ban be appealed?

Yes. Follow the appeal instructions in the Ban & Moderation Rules section. Include enough detail to identify the action, but never send your password or private authentication tokens.

Does Viral Cage Play guarantee prizes or tournament outcomes?

No prize, cash reward, official rating, tournament result or guaranteed matchmaking outcome is promised unless a separate official announcement clearly says otherwise.
